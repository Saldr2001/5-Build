Indie Film Collective

Project Description

Indie Film Collective is a multi-page website created to celebrate independent filmmaking and provide a space for creative individuals to connect, learn, and share their work.

The website includes four main pages: Home, Film Screenings, Workshops, and Collaborations.

Website Pages

Home: Introduces the organization and its mission.

Film Screenings: Highlights independent films and community screenings.

Workshops: Explores educational opportunities for aspiring filmmakers.

Collaborations: Encourages creative partnerships and community involvement.
